04 CASE STUDY

AMHARA BANK
ANNIVERSARY EVENT

IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE ADDIS ABABA, ETHIOPIA 2023
CLIENT Amhara Bank S.C.
LOCATION Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
YEAR 2023
SERVICES Experiential Architecture, Stage Production, Immersive Lighting & AV
AUDIENCE Shareholders, Banking Executives, Institutional Partners, Public Dignitaries
SCOPE Commemorative anniversary celebration, monumental scenic stage design, live orchestral audio, and banquet production.
01 CONTEXT

THE CHALLENGE

Commemorating a flagship banking institution's journey requires an experience that honors cultural heritage while articulating modern financial leadership. The event needed to orchestrate vast attendee numbers across dinner ceremonies, live performances, and high-level corporate addresses without losing emotional intimacy or momentum.

02 STRATEGY

THE SOLUTION

Eternal crafted a panoramic stage environment infused with warm architectural lighting, multi-layered visual scenography, and immersive acoustic dispersion. Custom cultural motifs were rendered in dynamic digital animations, providing an awe-inspiring backdrop for an unforgettable anniversary evening.

EXECUTION PHASES

OUR APPROACH

01

Heritage

Interweaving traditional iconography with modern architectural lines to celebrate corporate legacy.

02

Scenography

Curvilinear stage contours featuring multi-tiered displays with high-contrast cinematic content.

03

Illumination

Warm amber and golden washes creating a majestic, celebratory banquet environment.

04

Production

Integrated control surfaces directing live musical performances, stage cues, and protocol speeches.

05

Experience

An emotionally resonant atmosphere that united thousands of stakeholders in collective celebration.

06

Broadcast

Multicam recording, live feed broadcast switching, and cinematic post-event highlight reels.
