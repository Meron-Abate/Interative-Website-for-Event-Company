01 // CONTEXT

THE CHALLENGE

How do you unite sacred, ancestral Senegalese sabar drumming rituals with vanguard contemporary electronic choreography without diluting historical authenticity or feeling museum-like?

The physical setting also demanded uncompromising engineering: an open-air coastal clifftop amphitheater battered by aggressive Atlantic salt air, fluctuating coastal tides, and sudden wind gusts reaching 45 knots.

02 // STRATEGY

THE SOLUTION

We sculpted an amphitheater stage using local dark red volcanic stone and aerospace-grade brushed aluminum. Instead of fighting the Atlantic winds, we integrated them into the scenography: 14 giant kinetic tensile fabric sails caught the breeze, functioning simultaneously as wind harps, acoustic baffles, and dynamic projection surfaces.

Over 120 traditional sabar drummers, contemporary dancers, and modular synthesizer artists executed a 3-hour uninterrupted narrative arc that culminated in a massive collective communal ritual under the open sky.
