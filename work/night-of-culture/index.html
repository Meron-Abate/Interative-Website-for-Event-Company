01 CONTEXT

THE CHALLENGE

A historic luxury spirits house sought to engage East Africa's vanguard creative class without appearing commercial or heavy-handed. They required a nocturnal brand encounter where the brand's heritage felt like an organic patron of authentic cultural self-expression.

The challenge was to surgically convert an abandoned, dusty 4,000 sqm colonial railway hangar into an ethereal culinary and acoustic temple within 72 hours, leaving no architectural scar on the heritage monument.

02 STRATEGY

THE SOLUTION

We conceptualized 'The Chamber of Shadows' — treating darkness not as an absence of light, but as a sculpting material. We divided the monumental warehouse into four tactile chambers: Earth, Spirit, Rhythm, and Horizon.

Guests moved through raw basalt stone mixology bars, bespoke cast-bronze lighting fixtures, and custom acoustical fabric partitions. The night featured an unannounced intervention by an 18-piece brass ensemble paired with live analog synthesizers.
