02 CASE STUDY

Empowering Startups
in Ethiopia

Stage Design • Event Production • Show Design Addis Ababa • Ethiopia 2023
CLIENT Prime Minister’s Office
LOCATION Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
YEAR 2026
SERVICES Spatial Transformation, Sensory Gastronomy
AUDIENCE Startups, entrepreneurs, ecosystem stakeholders and government representatives
SCOPE Creative stage design and event production for a high-level gathering focused on strengthening Ethiopia’s startup ecosystem.

THE CHALLENGE

Ethiopia’s startup ecosystem was entering an important period of growth, with increasing attention on entrepreneurship, innovation and the systems needed to help startups scale. The event needed a setting that could give this emerging community a strong platform while supporting a high-level programme and its participants.

THE SOLUTION

Eternal developed an event environment that combined stage design, show design and production into one cohesive experience. The approach positioned the stage as the central visual element of the gathering while creating a professional setting for dialogue, presentations and engagement.

EXECUTION PHASES

OUR APPROACH

01

Strategy

We shaped the production around the event’s focus on entrepreneurship and innovation, creating a stage environment suited to a high-level gathering of startups and ecosystem stakeholders.

02

Concept

A contemporary event environment designed to place entrepreneurs and their ideas at the centre of the experience, giving the programme a strong and cohesive visual identity.

03

Design

Eternal developed the stage design and visual structure of the event, balancing presentation requirements with a distinctive production aesthetic that reflected the forward-looking nature of the gathering.

04

Production

72-hour surgical build within a sensitive historic industrial monument.

05

Experience

OThe finished environment created a professional platform for presentations, conversations and engagement, giving participants a setting that reflected the significance of Ethiopia’s emerging startup ecosystem.

06

Content

The event environment was designed to translate effectively across photography and event documentation, capturing the stage, speakers and wider gathering as part of the project’s visual record.
