03 CASE STUDY

SDG FINANCE
ACADEMY 2024

FORUM & SUMMIT ADDIS ABABA, ETHIOPIA 2024
CLIENT United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) & Partners
LOCATION Addis International Convention Center (AICC), Addis Ababa
YEAR 2024
SERVICES Equipment Rentals, Sound, Lighting & Staging Hardware, AV Production
AUDIENCE Institutional Investors, Finance Ministers, Multilateral Delegates
SCOPE Full-scale audiovisual infrastructure, stage lighting, acoustic line arrays, and hybrid recording.
01 CONTEXT

THE CHALLENGE

Convening multilateral financial institutions, sovereign funds, and policymakers to unlock scalable financing mechanisms for Sustainable Development Goals across Africa requires immaculate production. Technical interruptions or unclear acoustics directly hinder bilateral negotiations and high-stakes commitments.

02 STRATEGY

THE SOLUTION

Eternal delivered an integrated technical environment featuring calibrated acoustic engineering, synchronized multi-display digital scenography, stage architecture balancing formal diplomacy with dynamic presentation, and zero-latency live feeds.

EXECUTION PHASES

OUR APPROACH

01

Strategy

Aligning technical infrastructure with high-level multilateral protocol and bilingual breakout sessions.

02

Scenography

Stage architecture configured with high-lumen digital backdrops and dynamic architectural lighting.

03

Acoustics

Precision line array sound reinforcement ensuring pristine speech intelligibility across the expansive hall.

04

Production

Hardware deployment with redundant power systems, video switching, and multi-microphone matrix controls.

05

Experience

A frictionless atmosphere where delegates engage directly without acoustic strain or distractions.

06

Documentation

High-definition archival video capture and session photography preserving milestone commitments.
